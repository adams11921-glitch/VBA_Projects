## About the bulletin (how Park Road does things)
- Bulletin is a folded booklet: 7 x 8.5" pages on landscape legal paper, stapled in the middle.
- Sections, in order: cover, "A Reflection Before the Service" (with copyright lines), order of worship,
  "THIS WEEK at Park Road" schedule, "UPCOMING THIS MONTH" announcements.
- Hymns show the hymnal number at the right (#347). Songs not in the hymnal show the author instead, plus the words.
- Scripture reading ends with "Reader: The Word of the Lord" / "People: Thanks be to God!" (People line bold).

## About working with the add-in
- After any change: close Word, run build\Update-AddIn.ps1, reopen Word and test the button.
- The add-in was first written without being run in Word. Expect small fixes in the first sessions,
  and record each fix here so it isn't repeated.
