Making the Bulletin in Word

A replacement for Microsoft Publisher: what it does and how to use it

Microsoft is retiring Publisher, so we are moving the weekly bulletin to Microsoft Word. A small add-in puts a new Bulletin tab at the top of Word with buttons for everything in our bulletin. You work on the pages in reading order, and Word arranges them for folding and stapling when it prints, the same way Publisher did.

What it does

New BulletinStarts a complete bulletin in one click: a cover with a spot for a picture, A Reflection Before the Service, the order of worship and the announcement pages. Replace the [bracketed] text with this week's details.
Start Next WeekCopies last week's bulletin to a new file for next Sunday and updates the date everywhere. Last week's file is left unchanged.
Order of worshipButtons add a Worship Item, Hymn (title, verses, hymnal number and room for the words), Scripture (with Reader / People response), Responsive Reading, Sermon and a black Heading Bar, all already formatted.
AnnouncementsAnnouncement (Event | Date | Time | Place, then details), a Weekly Schedule table, and New Page.
Bulletin StylesSelect pasted text and choose a style (Lyrics, Chorus, People Line, Small Print…) so it matches the rest of the bulletin.
Booklet ViewShows two pages side by side, like the opened booklet.
Check PagesA folded booklet needs a multiple of 4 pages. Check Pages shows the page and sheet count and can add blank pages before the back cover.
PrintingPrint Booklet prints double-sided in folding order. PDF for Printing is for a copier or print shop, and PDF for Website gives one page per PDF page for the website and email.
Publisher tabFor flyers, posters and cards: text boxes and pictures that stay where you put them, linked text boxes, layout guides that don't print, align and arrange tools.

Installing (about one minute)

  1. Save the three files you were sent (WordPublisherKit.dotm, Install.cmd, Uninstall.cmd) into one folder. If they came in a .zip, right-click it and choose Extract All.
  2. Close Word. Then double-click Install.cmd and press any key when it says Installed.
  3. Open Word. A Bulletin tab now appears next to Home.

Needs Windows with Word 2013 or newer (Microsoft 365 is fine). To remove it, close Word and double-click Uninstall.cmd.

Everything moves to fit. The order of worship is ordinary text, so adding, removing or reordering items pushes the rest down or up. No boxes to resize or link.

Each kind of line has its own style:

  • Worship Item: bold heading, with optional text at the right edge
  • Hymn Line: title and verses, hymnal number at the right
  • Lyrics and Chorus (indented, chorus in italics)
  • Leader Line and People Line (the congregation's part in bold)
  • Scripture Text: italic, evenly justified

Pasting words from a website or email? Paste them, select them, then pick a style from Bulletin Styles so they match.

Change a style once and every line updates. For example, to make all hymn words larger, right-click Lyrics in Word's Styles list and choose Modify.

New Page starts the next item on a fresh page.

5 · How the booklet prints

You type the bulletin in reading order, page 1 to the end, at its finished size (7 × 8.5 inches). When you print, Word puts two pages on each side of a landscape legal sheet, in the order needed for the folded, stapled booklet.

Before printing: Check Pages

Check Pages
This bulletin has 14 pages = 4 sheets of paper.

A booklet needs a multiple of 4 pages, so 2 page(s) will be blank. Word would put them at the very end, after your back cover.

Add 2 blank page(s) just BEFORE the back cover instead?
YesNo
Each sheet holds 4 pages, so a booklet is always 4, 8, 12, 16… pages. Check Pages warns you and offers to add the blank pages inside the booklet so the back cover stays on the back. Use them for notes or more announcements.
Illustration of the Check Pages message.

Printing choices

  • Print Booklet: in the Print window choose Print on Both Sides, flip on short edge and Legal paper. If the printer can't print both sides, choose Manually Print on Both Sides and put the paper back in when Word asks.
  • PDF for Printing: saves the sheets exactly as above, for the office copier or a print shop.
  • PDF for Website: one page per PDF page, in reading order, for the website or email.

Each week, in short

  1. Open last week's bulletin and click Start Next Week. Confirm the date.
  2. Update the hymns, readings, sermon and announcements. Use the buttons to add items.
  3. Click Booklet View to look it over, then Check Pages.
  4. Print Booklet, or PDF for Printing for the copier, plus PDF for Website.
